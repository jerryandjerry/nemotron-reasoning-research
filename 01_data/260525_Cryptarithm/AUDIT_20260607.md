**Net on new constructs:** the merge table, drop-only witnesses, and delta-State are all sound (merge table: one
cosmetic incompleteness). Canonical-branch ordering is sound **except** the one `02e871e4` arbitrary pick, which is a
real determinacy bug to fix.

**Extractor reconciliation for the malformed boxes.** Per [`ANSWER_EXTRACTION.md`](../ANSWER_EXTRACTION.md), the
metric takes text after the last `\boxed{` and closes on the **last** `}` (`rfind('}')`, not the first), specifically
so answers containing `}` work. Under that rule all four brace-collision boxes recover their intended string:
`c922dbb4 → }{`, `e582df31 → }@`, `fcad9241 → }?`, `db66c42c → '}`. Step 5's "0 boxes extract empty" is therefore
correct. **On the record:** a naive `find('}')`-first extractor would return empty for `}{`, `}@`, `}?` — we rely on
the documented `rfind` behavior. These four are the corpus's most fragile renders; a one-line re-render would remove
all ambiguity (optional, cheap hardening).

## 5. Bottom line

The corpus is in strong shape: **72.3% of reasoning CoTs and 98.0% of format CoTs are perfect 10s**, estimated means
≈9.18 (reasoning) and ≈9.94 (format), and Step 5 confirms **0 empty extractions**. This session's new constructs
largely hold up — drop-only witnesses and delta-State are clean strengths, the merge table is sound modulo a cosmetic
incomplete-row issue, canonical ordering is sound except one case.

Three items are **not** rubber-stampable:

1. **`arithmetic_7681df4d` (reasoning 3) — REAL defect, must fix.** A fabricated EX5 verification substitutes a wrong
   operand (81 for 71) to fake a match that does not exist; EX5 genuinely fails (22≠12). This is answer-rationalization,
   exactly the failure mode the honesty rule forbids. One puzzle, but it is the one that matters.
2. **`little_endian_02e871e4` (reasoning 6) — REAL determinacy gap, should fix.** An unjustified F=6 pick (canonical-branch
   construct not applied) fixes a query operand when F=8 is equally valid; the boxed answer is not forced. Either justify
   the pick by a stated rule (matching how query-operator guessing is already handled) or flag indeterminacy.
3. **`mixed_concat_c922dbb4` (format 6) — REAL malformed-looking box.** `\boxed{}{}` reads as an empty box; it extracts
   correctly under the documented `rfind` metric, but it is the single most fragile render. Re-render to remove ambiguity.

A fourth, **systemic-but-cosmetic** item worth a coordinated phrasing fix (with user sign-off on the literal wording,
per the no-new-wording rule): the ~8 CoTs that print "X appears only in the query, so no reading can determine it" for a
symbol that was in fact pinned by all-different and *is* used by the QUERY. Harmless to every boxed answer, but a
recurring self-contradiction a reviewer will notice.

The `query_unseen_concat` family (9 CoTs, all 6) and the load-bearing "smallest" tiebreaks sit at the **edge of what
the rubric permits**: repeatable stated rules, so allowed, but the operand-order choice (a∥b vs b∥a) is a pure
unjustified default and the heuristic is admitted as a guess. Not defects today; they are the corpus's thinnest
legitimate reasoning and the first place to harden if the bar rises.

**Caveat repeated: this is single-reviewer coverage.** No finding here has a second opinion. Recommend a confirming pass
on at least `7681df4d`, `02e871e4`, `c922dbb4`, and the boilerplate set before treating the triage as final.
